### About me

I build AI systems that make it to production — not just notebooks.

- 🤖 I design **multi-agent systems** and **LLM-powered applications**, most recently at **Ayming** (Lyon), where I architected agent workflows that cut manual processes by ~40%.
- 👁️ Before that I built **real-time computer vision** at **Capgemini Engineering** (Casablanca) — road hazard detection with YOLOv8, SORT tracking and distance estimation.
- 🚀 Currently shipping an **AI job-application platform** (Spring Boot + Gemini) that tailors resumes, matches offers and automates recruiter outreach end to end.
- 🎓 MSc Data Science (Nexa Digital School, Lyon) · MSc Distributed Systems & AI (ENSET Mohammedia).
- 💬 Reach me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/othmansamih/) — happy to talk agents, RAG, or anything CV.
